About Elevate Builders

Every home begins with a promise.

A home is more than a place to live. It is where families gather, milestones are celebrated, and memories are made for years to come. At Elevate Builders, we believe buying a new home should be one of life’s most rewarding experiences.

Elevate Builders was founded in Northwest Arkansas with a clear purpose: to build well-crafted homes, make the buying process seamless, and earn the confidence of every homeowner we serve.

Every decision we make is guided by a simple philosophy: build each home with the same care, responsibility, and attention to detail we would want for our own families. That philosophy shapes our designs, our construction process, and the way we work with homeowners and business partners.

We don’t measure success by the number of homes we build. We measure it by the trust we earn, the relationships we establish, and the joy homeowners feel each time they come home.

As Elevate Builders grows, our focus remains unchanged: to build with integrity and strengthen the communities we serve. We look forward to building for you.

Scott BuckeyFounder & President

Investor Program

Invest in new-construction homes with Elevate Builders.

Elevate Builders partners with individual investors, family offices, and institutions to develop single-family homes in Northwest Arkansas, the nation’s ninth-fastest-growing metro, where newly built properties account for more than a third of all sales. Investors retain full ownership of the asset and its realized returns at disposition, while Elevate Builders delivers each home from site selection to completion under a guaranteed maximum price.

The program offers direct exposure to sustained for-sale and rental demand without the operational burden of design, permitting, trade management, and scheduling. Commitments can range from a single home to a multi-home portfolio, with the flexibility to sell at completion or hold for long-term rental income.

The Investment Process

  1. Underwriting

    Review the Opportunity

    Each opportunity begins with a vetted homesite in a high-demand Northwest Arkansas neighborhood and a project-specific pro forma covering land basis, hard and soft costs, financing assumptions, projected sale price or rental income, and estimated hold period.

  2. Acquisition & Capitalization

    Commit & Finance

    The homesite is acquired, financing is put in place, and a guaranteed maximum price (GMP) contract with a defined contingency is signed.

  3. Pre-Construction

    Plan & Permit

    Plans, specifications, approvals, and permits are finalized, so work begins with a set scope, budget, and schedule.

  4. Construction

    Build & Fund Draws

    The build is scheduled for approximately five months, with bi-weekly draws tied to completed milestones and progress reports at each stage.

  5. Disposition

    Sell or Hold

    At completion, you choose the exit: sell the home and distribute net profit to the ownership entity, or hold it as a long-term rental.

Roles & Responsibilities

The Investor

  • Project capitalization (equity, debt, or both)
  • Financing costs, including origination and interest, if leveraged
  • Builder’s risk insurance
  • Funding bi-weekly construction draws
  • Disposition costs, including commissions and closing costs
  • Receives net profit at sale, or net operating income (NOI) if held

Elevate Builders

  • Homesite identification and evaluation
  • Pre-construction planning and design
  • Design approvals and building permits
  • Construction management and trade coordination
  • GMP contract, with cost overruns absorbed
  • Builder fee included in the GMP
  • Progress reporting through completion

Preferred Real Estate Broker

  • Homesite sourcing
  • Market and pricing guidance
  • Listing, marketing, and sale of the finished home

Program Safeguards & Alignment

Capped Construction Exposure

Your exposure is capped at the guaranteed maximum price. Elevate Builders absorbs overruns beyond it, subject to the terms of the GMP contract, so the contracted budget is your maximum hard-cost commitment.

Shared Contingency Savings

Unused contingency is shared 60% to the investor and 40% to Elevate Builders, aligning incentives around efficient construction.

Tailored Structure

Each project can be held through the investment vehicle of your choosing, structured to align with your goals and objectives.

Investor Package

Every investment is supported by a full underwriting package, including:

  • Project-specific pro forma
  • Sample GMP contractor agreement
  • Market comparables and pricing analysis
  • Sale and hold (rental) exit scenarios
  • Company overview
